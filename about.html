The path

How I got from rooms to screens.

Interior design taught me how people interact with spaces, and how visual design can support their needs.

Over time I saw that websites and apps need the same thinking: thoughtful layouts, clear information and an understanding of user behavior. That led me to an M.S. in User Experience and Interaction Design at Touro University. I see UX/UI as a natural extension of my design background, and I want to combine visual design with user-centered problem solving.

Where I started

Soaring Wing Adoption Center

Bachelor's thesis · New York School of Interior Design · December 2024 · 150 Bay Street, Jersey City · Enscape, Photoshop, Lightroom

An adoption center, after-school daycare and pet therapy space for American-Chinese orphans, inside a 1908 warehouse building.

Children are like newborn birds: they need help to learn to fly. A spiral stair and a "break through to the sky" axis tie the floors together, and pet therapy, aquarium walls, plants and natural materials support emotional healing.

Accessibility was a requirement from the start, so people using wheelchairs appear in the renders.
